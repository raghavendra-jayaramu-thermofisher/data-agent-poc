# Thermo Fisher APJ — Fiscal Calendar FY2026

## Calendar structure

- **Fiscal year:** January 1–December 31, 2026.
- **Fiscal week:** Sunday–Saturday, except partial boundary weeks: **W1** (January 1–3) and **W53** (December 27–31).
- **Fiscal quarters:** Q1–Q3 contain 13 weeks each; Q4 contains 14 weeks (W40–W53).
- **Fiscal months:** Follow a **5–4–4 week** pattern in Q1–Q3 and **5–4–5** in Q4. Their boundaries do not always match calendar months.

## Important boundaries

- **Quarter starts:** Q1 January 1; Q2 March 29; Q3 June 28; Q4 September 27.
- **Fiscal periods may cross calendar months:** For example, fiscal April starts March 29, and fiscal October starts September 27.
- **Year-specific reference:** These boundaries apply to FY2026; do not assume identical dates in other fiscal years.
